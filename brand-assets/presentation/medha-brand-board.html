07 — Symbol Design Rationale

The M-Graph Mark

The MEDHA symbol is a geometric knowledge graph in which five nodes connect through directed edges to form the silhouette of the letter M. The five nodes represent five cognitive states in a learning journey: beginning knowledge, two emerging understanding peaks, one reflective mid-point valley, and mastery.

  • Indigo (#6366F1) edges — The primary learning path: structured, deliberate progression through concepts.
  • Cyan (#22D3EE) dashed connectors — Associative links: how MEDHA surfaces hidden cross-concept relationships.
  • Teal valley node (#14B8A6) — The adaptive inflection point: where MEDHA diagnoses misconceptions and re-routes.
  • Cyan terminal nodes — Entry and mastery states: where a student begins and where they arrive.

The dashed cyan connectors are intentionally subtle — they signal intelligence working beneath the surface, not noise. The mark scales from 16×16 favicon to billboard without losing its geometry. The letter M is structural, not decorative.
